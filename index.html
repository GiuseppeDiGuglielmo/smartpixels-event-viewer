About this view

Dataset: —

Charge is pixelAV simulated output in electrons, cumulative over 20 time slices. The line is the event's truth track: straight, with no field, scattering or energy loss.

Pixel addresses are zero-based (x 0–47, y 0–191). The pixel axis counts from the sensor centre. pixelAV (x, y) origin: centre of pixel (23, 95), see docs/PIXELAV_DATA.md.
